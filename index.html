A special military operation

The Orc Overlord

We declare war on you. This is a preemptive strike, carried out strictly in self-defense. You were working with our enemies. You were plotting against us. You were about to strike first, the evidence is irrefutable, history is on our side, and we had no other choice. According to orc history, your country has no right to exist anyway, and the land belongs to orcs. And so on, and so forth.

A rules-based partnership

The Allied Leader

The Orc Overlord's act of aggression has infringed international law. We looked it up. The allies will therefore help you defend your country and your people, a position we have held consistently, and which we are pleased to repeat for anyone who may have missed it the first several times. The allies send you helmets, because the protection of your people begins, as our experts have explained to us, with something a person can wear. The allies send you a patriot system, a contribution of real substance, and one we would ask you not to bury in the middle of the story when you tell it later. The allies send you two hundred and fifty gold, which is not nothing, and five air-to-air missiles, which are also not nothing, and which we would like entered into the record as generosity. You had better be very thankful. Thankfulness is the part of this arrangement that does not have to be shipped.

A limited nuclear operation

The Orc Overlord

You have had four turns to surrender, and you spent them throwing rocks. How disappointing. We are therefore obliged, purely in self-defense, to introduce our nuclear rockets: sixty-seven of them, aimed at you, your allies, your king, and whatever is left of the hillside. Surrender, and this remains a stern conversation. Refuse, and we will annihilate every one of you and describe it later as a limited operation. The button is polished. Our patience is not.
